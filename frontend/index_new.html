History & Significance of IOCL and Guwahati Refinery

Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) is India’s largest government-owned oil & gas enterprise, operating across refining, pipelines, fuel marketing, and petrochemicals. The Guwahati Refinery serves as a critical energy hub for the Northeast, supporting civilian infrastructure and strategic mobility.

  • Commissioned on January 1, 1962 as India’s first public sector refinery.
  • Established with international collaboration and inaugurated by national leadership.
  • Produces LPG, petrol (MS), diesel (HSD), kerosene, and other fuels for regional demand.
  • Strengthens regional energy security and reduces dependence on imported fuels.
  • Supports national defense logistics by ensuring reliable fuel availability for operations and training.

About IOCL

India's flagship national oil company and a Fortune 500 enterprise

Indian Oil Corporation Limited

Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) is India's largest commercial enterprise, with a sales turnover of ₹6,95,088 crore and profits of ₹26,371 crore for the fiscal year 2021-22. IOCL is the highest ranked Indian company in the Fortune Global 500 listing.

The Corporation, together with its subsidiaries, accounts for 48.83% of India's refining capacity and 32.25% of petroleum products market share. IOCL operates 11 of India's 23 refineries with a combined refining capacity of 80.70 MMTPA.
